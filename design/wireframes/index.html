How to read these

  • Greyscale only. Solid dark = loaded, approved, on plan. Hatched = partial, pending, beyond TAT. Dashed = missing or empty.
  • Every figure is DEMO. Invented for layout; nothing here is Virgo data.
  • Growth is QoQ only. Every comparison is with the previous quarter at the same working-day count. There is no last-year option anywhere.
  • Every KPI card and table shows Potential · Target · Actual · Var · Ach % · Share · Last Qtr · QoQ % · Trend; charts show actual bars, a plan line and a previous-quarter marker.
  • Resize the window to 360 px and 1440 px. Below 860 px the sidebar becomes a top strip. Tables scroll inside their frame, never the page.
  • Labels marked "pending rules" wait for the Data Architect's files (KRA/KPI weights, A/B/C cut-offs, ageing edges, potential definitions for non-sales KPIs).
