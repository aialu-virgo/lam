# Laminates business
- Virgo Group laminates business: manufacture at factories, sell through branches and depots; head office (HO) coordinates. Sites: [Branches and plants](branches-plants.md).
- Financial year April–March; Q1 = Apr–Jun.
- Quantities in sheets and sq ft (item-master conversion); values in ₹, shown in lakh / crore with Indian grouping.
- Departments in scope: sales, sales / CRR coordination, accounts (receivables, collections), plants, HO management. Roles: [Role matrix](../design/role-matrix.md).
- Transactions live in SAP Business One today, S/4HANA later: [SAP Business One](../systems/sap-b1.md), [SAP S/4HANA](../systems/sap-s4hana.md).
- Open: customer segments, product groups, market-potential source — see [Open questions](../open-questions.md).
