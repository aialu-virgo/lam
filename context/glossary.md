# Glossary
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Market potential | Addressable business per quarter × branch/territory × product group × segment, in ₹ and quantity |
| Share % | Target share of market potential |
| Target | Market potential × share % (see [Target model](../design/target-model.md)) |
| Share of potential | Actual ÷ potential |
| Achievement % | Actual ÷ current approved target |
| QoQ | Growth vs the previous quarter — the only comparison basis ([Comparison basis](../design/comparison-basis.md)) |
| Stretch | Target needing more than 2× the 12-quarter average QoQ growth |
| Today cap | Maximum actions on a person's Today list (role default) |
| L1 / L2 / L3 | Escalation steps ([Escalation ladder](../design/escalation-ladder.md)) |
| O2D / TAT | Order-to-dispatch days / planned turnaround time |
| CRR | Customer relations coordination |
| HO / HOD | Head office / head of department |
| B1 / S4 | SAP Business One (now) / SAP S/4HANA (later) |
| Closed quarter | A historic quarter, locked; reload needs Super admin |
| DEMO | Synthetic data, labelled and removable |
