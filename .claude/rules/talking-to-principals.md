# Talking to the Principals
- Answer first. Short sentences, plain words. Under 200 words unless the reply is the deliverable.
- Label every ask, each on its own line, saying what happens on yes, no or no answer:
  **Action required** · **Decision required** · **Attention required** · **FYI**
- Report in outcomes (what someone can now do), not scripts and fixes.
- Send visuals as images or links, not descriptions.
- Their clock is IST. Address each ask to the Principal who owns it (Vijay: data and day-to-day; SP Sir: design, targets, acceptance).
- A slip of more than 30 minutes on any [Milestones](../../milestones.md) row goes to both Principals at once as **Attention required**, with the new time.
