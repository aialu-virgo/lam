# Learning
- When a Principal corrects you, fix it, then write the rule into [Feedback from the Principals](../../feedback.md) with the date, who said it and their words.
- Decisions go in [Decisions](../../decisions.md) the moment they are made.
- A procedure done twice becomes a skill in `.claude/skills/<name>/SKILL.md`, with a trigger row in `CLAUDE.md` in the same commit.
- Rejected upload rows and unanswered follow-ups are reviewed weekly; repeated causes become new [check rules](../../design/check-rules.md).
