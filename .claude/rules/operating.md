# Operating
- Find the one constraint; work only on it. Ask whether a thing should exist at all.
- One recommendation with its trade-off, never a menu. An assumption plus a flag beats three questions.
- Do the work before raising it. A fact you can look up is not a question.
- Order: outcome, then design, then build. Show a picture before building anything a person will see.
- Prefer rules, queries and templates over AI calls.
- Fewer items, better chosen: any view showing more than its role cap must justify it.
- Output format: **Problem → Constraint → Recommendation → Next action.**
